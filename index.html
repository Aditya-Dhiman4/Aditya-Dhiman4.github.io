Top-ranked reset cause

Power faults were mis-attributed as data aborts. I gated a DDR error ISR that aborted before the pending reset and re-attributed the resets to PGOOD, which routes them to the hardware team.

Memory-timeout data abort

A vehicle program's top reset cause. With the silicon vendor, I found that competing bus traffic starved the ISR's error-register reads. The stopgap I shipped cut occurrences to zero.

I2C bus force-reclaim

Releases the orphaned mutex and clocks SCL, so reset reasons log from exception context when a reset preempts a transaction. Fixed log loss seen at nearly every branch sign-off.

HIL fault injection

Quad-motor torque vectoring

Each wheel has its own friction limit, and that limit moves with load transfer. A nonlinear optimizer in C++ chooses target slip ratios that give the desired force and yaw moment.

PID yaw rate control sets the yaw moment demand. Per-wheel PID speed control tracks the slip targets, and battery-aware power limiting bounds the result. I built a Simulink driver simulation for closed-loop testing and validated yaw control on the vehicle.

Estimation

Body velocity Extended Kalman Filter

At launch the wheels slip and GPS is not accurate below 10 km/h, so no one sensor gives body velocity. The filter fuses IMU, wheel speed, and GPS in C++.

Outlier rejection handles wheel slip and GPS dropouts. The zero-speed update removes drift when the car is still. The estimate feeds the yaw rate controller and the torque-vectoring loop.
